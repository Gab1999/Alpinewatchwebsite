What are glaciers?

Glaciers are large masses of ice that form over thousands of years from accumulated snow. They are found in mountainous regions and polar areas. Specific climate conditons are necessary for glaciers to exist for as long as they have with most glaciers existing longer than most civilisations.

Why should we care?

Glaciers are millennia old and are only now beggining to crumble and melt at an alarming rate due to climate change. This affects water supplies, ecosystems and surrounding communities but also serves as a drastic reminder that climate change is no hoax or conspiracy. Glaciers have also been traversed by us humans for a long time however it is becoming increasingly dangerous due to new crevases forming yearly.
