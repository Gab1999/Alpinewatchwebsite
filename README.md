My chosen topic for my informative website is the destruction of glaciers through climate change and the effect that has on local communities, ecosystems and more. The name fo the website will be AlpineWatch. 

I am still deciding on design choices in terms of how I can creatively display information. One idea I had was to potentially have a digital-interactive globe with all/most glaciers represented in their respective geographical locations where if a user clicks on one of the glaciers, information comes up on its age, history and level of ablation (loss of mass due to melting) among other environmental statistics. I am not sure how hard/easy that would be to implement but I have seen this type of “Interactive globe” before. 


DataSets:

I will be implementing data from:

The European Environment Agency (https://www.eea.europa.eu/en/datahub) 

Eurostat (https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/main/home) 

The Climate Change Indicators Dashboard (https://climatedata.imf.org/pages/access-data)
